Our process

Four phases to production-ready AI

Each phase has a clear deliverable. No black boxes, no surprises.

  1. 01
    Phase one

    Discover

    We map your workflows, identify the highest-impact AI opportunities, and select the use case with the best value-to-effort ratio. You walk away with a concrete implementation plan.

    • Workflow audit
    • Opportunity scoring
    • Implementation plan
    OutputA ranked roadmap & one chosen use case
  2. 02
    Phase two

    Prototype

    In 1–2 weeks we build a working proof-of-concept on your real data. Stakeholders can see and test it immediately — no hand-waving, no slideware.

    • Working PoC
    • Real data
    • Live demo
    Timeline10 days from kickoff to clickable demo
  3. 03
    Phase three

    Refine

    We iterate based on feedback, harden edge cases, add guardrails and evals, and tune performance. The prototype evolves into something production-worthy.

    • Eval suites
    • Guardrails
    • Latency & cost tuning
    OutputA version your security team will sign off on
  4. 04
    Phase four

    Deploy

    We integrate the solution into your existing systems, train your team to maintain it, and hand over full documentation. You own everything — including the code.

    • Integration
    • Team training
    • Full handover
    OutputA production system your team owns and runs
