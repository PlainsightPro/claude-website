Our process

Four phases to production-ready AI

1. Discover

We map your workflows, identify the highest-impact AI opportunities, and select the use case with the best value-to-effort ratio. You walk away with a concrete implementation plan.

2. Prototype

In 1–2 weeks, we build a working proof-of-concept. Real data, real results. Stakeholders can see and test it immediately — no hand-waving required.

3. Refine

We iterate based on feedback, harden edge cases, add guardrails, and optimize performance. The prototype evolves into something production-worthy.

4. Deploy

We integrate the solution into your existing systems, train your team to maintain it, and hand over full documentation. You own everything.

Our approach
